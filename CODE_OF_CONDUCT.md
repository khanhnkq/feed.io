# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Feed.io a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level or role.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while asking for evidence.
- Accept responsibility, apologize and repair harm.
- Protect private information and security reports.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing and sustained disruption are not tolerated in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer. Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation or permanently ban a participant based on impact and context. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy is based on the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
